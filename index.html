Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver medicines?

Yes, we deliver medicines across all areas of Bhubaneswar. You can place your order through our website, WhatsApp, or by calling us. We ensure timely and safe delivery at your doorstep.

How do I book a blood test?

You can book a blood test by filling our "Book Diagnostic Test" form on the website, or you can contact us directly on WhatsApp or phone. Choose your preferred test and collection method, and our team will contact you to confirm the appointment.

Can I upload my prescription?

Yes, absolutely! You can upload your prescription while placing an order through our website. You can also send your prescription via WhatsApp or bring it when you visit our store.

What are your working hours?

We are open daily from 8:00 AM to 10:30 PM. You can visit our store, call us, or place orders anytime during these hours.

How will I receive my diagnostic reports?

Your diagnostic reports are delivered through WhatsApp for quick and convenient access. We ensure timely report generation and secure delivery to your registered WhatsApp number.

Is the home sample collection safe?

Yes, our home sample collection is completely safe and hygienic. We use trained and certified phlebotomists who follow all medical protocols and use sterile equipment for sample collection.

What is the delivery time for medicines?

We strive to deliver medicines within 2-4 hours during our working hours. For emergency medicines, we offer expedited delivery. Contact us for specific delivery requirements.

Do you provide emergency medicine support?

Yes, we provide emergency medicine support during our business hours (8 AM - 10:30 PM). Call or WhatsApp us immediately with your requirements, and we'll do our best to fulfill your urgent medicine needs.
